Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

The user picked three round-3 emblems to refine: **1 Offbeat star**,
**3 Ghost keyboard** and **6 Two-key L** (logo-ideas/emblem-1.svg, -3, -6).
Keep everything from logo-ideas/codex-brief-3.md: no faces, no text, one
monoline outline in `currentColor`, round joins and caps, the lively tilt of

Make **three variants of each** (9 files):
logo-ideas/refined-1a.svg, 1b, 1c (star), 3a, 3b, 3c (keyboard), 6a, 6b, 6c (L).
Add logo-ideas/refined.md: one line per variant on what changed.

## Across all three
- Get closer to the bow's craft: corners softened like the bow's (straight
  sides that meet in small radii, not sharp points or plain rectangles), a
  deliberate off-centre balance, and one small detail that makes the shape
  feel hand-made (like the bow's square knot or notched tails).
- Stroke about 28-32 at 512, consistent within a mark.
- Must read clearly at 16 px. Render at 512, 32 and 16 and fix any variant
  where lines touch or counters close up.

## 1 Offbeat star (red, #d6455a)
Currently a plain tilted outline star, clean but generic. Give it character
without losing the simplicity: e.g. a faceted star (a short inner line or two
suggesting a fold, like origami or a cut gem), one point drawn longer like a
flourish or the start of a stroke of music, a small open gap in the outline
like a breath, or a star whose tip curls into a little tail. Keep it a star.

## 3 Ghost keyboard (teal, #1f6f78)
Currently five keys inside a frame; the lines merge at small sizes. Simplify:
fewer keys (two or three white keys with one or two black keys), no full
outer frame or an open one, bigger gaps, so it still says "piano keys"
floating at an angle. A variant may lift one key slightly, as if pressed by
someone unseen.

## 6 Two-key L (teal, #1f6f78)
Currently an L outline with interior lines that clog. Keep the idea (piano
keys forming her initial) but make the L itself the strong shape: one clean
L outline with at most one black key or one dividing line, generous counters,
softened corners. A variant may set the black key as the L's crossing point.
