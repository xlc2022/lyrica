# Lyrica emblem proposals

Round 3 serves principle 9: character expressed through a signature object. Angular outlines, open interiors and a slight tilt take their style from the supplied reference; no reference artwork is included. These are proposals only, with no application or contract changes.

1. **Offbeat star** — [emblem-1.svg](emblem-1.svg): a broad, tilted five-point ornament with deliberately uneven points, in warm red.
2. **Star cap** — [emblem-2.svg](emblem-2.svg): a sloping, straight-sided hat with a five-point star at its crown, in warm red.
3. **Ghost keyboard** — [emblem-3.svg](emblem-3.svg): three open white keys and two outlined raised keys, floating at an angle, in teal.
4. **Encore star** — [emblem-4.svg](emblem-4.svg): a single shooting-star ornament with two long, unequal trails, in warm red.
5. **Star key** — [emblem-5.svg](emblem-5.svg): the star ornament becomes the head of one long piano key, in teal.
6. **Two-key L** — [emblem-6.svg](emblem-6.svg): two oversized, perpendicular piano keys share an L-shaped outline, in teal.

All six use a uniform 26-unit stroke with round caps and joins, transparent interiors and `currentColor`. Each was rendered at 512, 32 and 16 px; scratch previews are under `/tmp/lyrica-emblems/`.
