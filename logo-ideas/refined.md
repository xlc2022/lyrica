# Refined emblems

Proposals for round 4, serving principle 1 (experience before infrastructure): recognizable, personal marks at favicon size. All use a 30-unit currentColor outline with softened corners; no new UI controls.

- **1a — Long note:** stretches the upper point and rounds each turn into short, straight-sided facets; the low left point keeps the balance uneven.
- **1b — Breath:** opens the upper-right shoulder with widely separated round caps, retaining five points and a long leading tip.
- **1c — Paper star:** adds one short, detached fold inside a broad counter, with an extended lower-right point and a slight tilt.
- **3a — Quiet interval:** reduces the keyboard to two broad keys under an open top, with one short black-key stroke above the divider.
- **3b — Unseen touch:** separates two open keys and lifts the right one, leaving one suspended black-key stroke between them.
- **3c — Three voices:** keeps three white keys and two staggered black-key strokes, removing the top rail and leaving generous vertical gaps.
- **6a — Head key:** softens a broad L and places its single short black-key stroke at the top of the upright.
- **6b — Corner key:** makes the L's elbow the only key division, with a longer right foot and a livelier tilt.
- **6c — Foot key:** moves the single short black-key stroke onto the foot, preserving the large open elbow and a quieter tilt.

Rendered and visually checked at 512, 32 and 16 px; scratch previews remain under `/tmp/lyrica-refined/`. Joined key baselines and the L division are intentional; detached strokes retain open gaps. These are standalone proposals, so no component, contract or interface documentation changes are needed.
