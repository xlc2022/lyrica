# Lyrica logo proposals

These serve principle 9, “The persona is about her, not about the machine”: six glyphs drawn from her music, judgment and care for lost sounds. Proposals only; no app changes.

1. **Key turn** — Three piano keys, the first turning into an L: the arranger gives a small keyboard its direction. Teal; single colour.
2. **The lost note** — A broad red star shelters a musical note cut out of its centre: a sound kept safe in a small spark of mischief. Single colour, with transparent negative space.
3. **Nobody at the piano** — An off-kilter black hat floats above one short dash of a piano key; a red brim supplies the theatrical wink. A performance with no visible performer, without borrowing a character costume.
4. **Asterisk with intent** — A stout six-armed footnote star: the overlooked detail promoted to the main mark. Teal; single colour, deliberately as simple as a stamp.
5. **The arranger** — Two curved outer voices meet a steady teal middle voice and finish as one stem: three temperaments brought to a shared conclusion. Charcoal and teal; also works with both set to one colour.
6. **Keep this sound** — A bookmark with a note cut through it: an index entry for the sound, reason or detail nobody else filed. Teal; single colour, with transparent negative space.
