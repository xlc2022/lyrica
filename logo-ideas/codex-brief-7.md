# Codex brief, round 7: refine moons 1 and 4

Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

The user picked **moon 1 Quiet crescent** (teal outline) and **moon 4 Night
companion** (gold crescent with a four-point star) from round 6
(logo-ideas/moon-1.svg, moon-4.svg). Keep the round 6 rules
(logo-ideas/codex-brief-6.md): single colour via `currentColor`, round caps
and joins, no faces, no text, 512 viewBox, the confident simplicity and
slight tilt of Monika's bow. Must read at 16 px.

Make **three variants of each** (6 files): logo-ideas/moon-1a.svg, 1b, 1c
and moon-4a.svg, 4b, 4c. Add logo-ideas/moon-refined.md with one line per
variant on what changed.

## 1 Quiet crescent
Keep it an outline crescent. Explore the proportions: a slimmer or fuller
crescent, the tips' shape (rounded vs. a softened point like the bow's
corners), stroke weight (about 28-36), the amount of tilt. One variant may
add one small hand-made detail in the bow's spirit, such as a slight gap
where the inner and outer curves meet at one tip, as long as it stays quiet.

## 4 Night companion
The star turns to a speck at 16 px. Fix that first: make the star larger
and closer to the crescent, tuck it into the crescent's hollow, or cut it
out of a filled crescent as negative space. Try at least one outline
version that matches moon 1's line weight, so 1 and 4 could live together
as a pair. Keep the star four- or five-pointed (five points nods to the
star on her seed's hat). Gold #c9a227 or teal #1f6f78.
