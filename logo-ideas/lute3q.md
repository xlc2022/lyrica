# Three-quarter lutes

Six standalone proposals for brief 5; principle 1, experience before infrastructure, guides the emphasis on a recognisable mark at small sizes. No interface controls or application changes.

1. **Sweep** — A single open back curve gives the teal bowl depth beneath a narrowed face and sharply folded pegbox.
2. **Fan** — One interior rib splits the warm-red back into two broad bands, keeping the construction visible without fine hatching.
3. **Shadow** — A solid teal crescent carries the bowl volume while the face, ring rose and bridge remain outlined.
4. **Star turn** — A broader face and shallow back put an open five-point rose inside a briskly tilted warm-red lute.
5. **Counterturn** — The teal bowl turns the opposite way, exposing its left back beneath a rightward pegbox bend.
6. **Near neck** — A tapered, foreshortened neck and compact bent pegbox sit above a ribbed warm-red bowl.

All use a 512 × 512 viewBox, a 24-unit currentColor stroke, round caps and joins, and the root teal or warm red colour. Shadow alone uses the filled crescent permitted by brief 5. No strings or peg details compete with the silhouette.

Rendered and inspected at 512, 32 and 16 px; the star centre was opened after the first small-size check. At 16 px the silhouette and bowl division carry recognition; the rose and bridge are small accents. Scratch renders are in /tmp/lute3q-review/ and are not deliverables. The read-only style reference was inspected in place and was not copied into the repository.
