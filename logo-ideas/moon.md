# Moon concepts

Proposals for brief 6, serving principle 1, “Experience before infrastructure,” through a recognizable companion identity. Each mark uses one colour and a slight tilt; no interface controls are added.

1. **Quiet crescent** — A teal outline with a broad, open hollow and rounded tips.
2. **Nightkeeper** — A solid warm-red crescent, the simplest and strongest silhouette.
3. **Breath** — One open teal stroke curls into a crescent without quite closing.
4. **Night companion** — A gold crescent shelters one small four-point star.
5. **Resonance** — A warm-red outline crescent whose inner curve suggests a lute’s bowl and neck.
6. **Turning** — A gold moon with one curved phase line, tilted gently off vertical.

All six were rendered and visually checked at 512, 32 and 16 px. Scratch PNGs and the comparison sheet are in `/tmp/lyrica-moons/`; the reference remains outside the repository.
