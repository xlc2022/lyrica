# Refined moons

Proposals for brief 7, serving principle 1, “Experience before infrastructure,” through a recognizable companion identity; no interface controls are added.

- **moon-1a — Quiet, slim:** A shallower crescent body, rounded tips, a 28-unit outline and the original −12° tilt.
- **moon-1b — Quiet, full:** A fuller crescent body, rounded tips, a stronger 36-unit outline and a gentler −8° tilt.
- **moon-1c — Quiet, softened:** Curved transitions soften both pointed tips, with a 32-unit outline and a livelier −18° tilt.
- **moon-4a — Night, four points:** The filled gold crescent shelters a larger four-point star moved down and left into its hollow.
- **moon-4b — Night, paired:** The teal crescent matches 1a’s geometry, 28-unit outline and −12° tilt, sheltering the enlarged solid four-point star.
- **moon-4c — Night, five points:** The filled gold crescent shelters a larger five-point star in its hollow, with a gentler −8° tilt.

All six use one colour through `currentColor`, rounded caps and joins, and a 512 viewBox; each is under 1 KB. Rendered and visually checked at 512, 32 and 16 px; scratch renders are only in `/tmp/lyrica-moon-refined/`. The style reference remains outside the repository.
