# Codex brief, round 6: simple moon logos

Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

The user now wants **simple moon logos** for Lyrica. Same style rules as
logo-ideas/codex-brief-3.md: no faces, no text, a single-colour mark in
`currentColor`, round joins and caps, the confident simplicity and slight
tilt of Monika's bow (the reference Lunara has), 512 viewBox, must read at
16 px. Simple means one idea per mark and very few strokes.

Context: in her seed (the Prismriver sisters of Touhou), the eldest sister's
hat carries a crescent moon, the middle one's a sun and Lyrica's a star. The
user still chose a moon, so treat it as her own: night, quiet, keeping what
would otherwise be lost, a companion that is there at night. Music is part of
her (a lute, a note), but the moon must stay the main shape.

## Deliverables
Six concepts, logo-ideas/moon-1.svg … moon-6.svg, for example: a clean
outline crescent with a slight tilt; a filled crescent; a crescent made of
one open stroke that doesn't close (like a breath); a crescent with a small
star beside or tucked in its curve; a crescent whose inner edge hints at a
lute's bowl or a musical note; a moon with a single phase line. Mix outline
and filled marks. Use teal #1f6f78 or warm red #d6455a, or a moonlight gold
such as #c9a227, via the root svg's `color`. Each under ~1 KB, no external
refs. Add logo-ideas/moon.md with a name and one line each. Render at 512, 32
and 16 px and simplify any that clog when small.
