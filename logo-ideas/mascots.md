# Mascot concepts

Proposals serving principle 9: her identity comes from her character. Flat teal `#1f6f78`, warm red `#d95149`, near-black `#24232b` and white; transparent backgrounds, no new interface controls.

1. **Quiet Plot** — A winking poltergeist in a tiny red-banded hat, with a scalloped floating hem and a knowing grin.
2. **Lost Song** — A round songbird in a little musician’s hat, bright-eyed and smiling as it keeps a tune to itself.
3. **Good Scheme** — A cat with one narrowed eye and a pleased little muzzle; one teal ink with transparent facial cutouts.
4. **After Hours** — A friendly bat with broad scalloped wings, a lopsided grin and one red collar diamond.
5. **Night Listener** — A moth spirit with red wings, listening antennae and a cheeky sideways smile.
6. **Small Mischief** — A soft horned imp with a wink and a delighted grin; one near-black ink with transparent facial cutouts.

All six were rendered and inspected at 512, 32 and 16 px. Concepts 3 and 6 are already single-colour marks: changing their one fill preserves the complete face, including on a dark background. Renders are scratch files under `/tmp`.
