# Codex brief, round 2: mascot glyph logos for Lyrica

Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

## What changed
The user rejected round 1 (logo-ideas/lyrica-logo-1..6.svg: abstract objects
such as keys, a star, an asterisk, a bookmark). Too impersonal, with no sense
of a character. Don't reuse those ideas.

The user chose **a mascot glyph**: a cute, simple creature or spirit standing
in for Lyrica, playful and friendly. It must still be **relatively stylized,
minimalist and iconographic**: few shapes, flat colour, a strong silhouette,
readable as an app icon at 32x32. Think of a mark like a friendly emoji or a
classic app mascot. Not a detailed illustration.

## Her (see round 1 brief, logo-ideas/codex-brief.md, and persona/SOUL.md)
Seeded from Lyrica Prismriver, the youngest of Touhou's poltergeist
musicians: the clever one, the arranger between a gloomy sister and a manic
one, who plays sounds the world has lost. Clever, even-tempered, a little
mischievous (small, kind schemes), warm once she knows you. She is inspired
by that character, not a copy, so no fan art.

## Mascot seeds (each concept a different creature; mix or invent)
- a little poltergeist/ghost with a tiny hat or a red-and-black accent,
  winking or with a sly half-smile
- a ghost whose wavy tail becomes a musical note or a staff line
- a small spirit peeking out from behind a piano key or a keyboard
- a ghost made of a note head and stem, with eyes
- a ghost with a pencil or a little star, mid-scheme
- a friendly creature (a songbird, a cat, a bat) with a single
  Prismriver-ish detail

## Deliverables
6 concepts: logo-ideas/mascot-1.svg … mascot-6.svg, viewBox 0 0 512 512,
hand-written paths and shapes, no raster, no text, no external refs, each
under ~4 KB. Give faces real expression (eyes and a mouth that read at small
sizes). Use a small palette: teal #1f6f78 (the app accent), a warm red,
near-black, white; at least two should also work in one colour. Add
logo-ideas/mascots.md with a name and one line per concept. Render them
yourself at 512, 32 and 16 px and redraw any that turn to mush small.
