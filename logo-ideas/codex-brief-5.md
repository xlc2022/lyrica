# Codex brief, round 5: three-quarter view lutes

Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

The user now wants Lyrica's mark to be a **lute seen in three-quarter
view**: turned so the face narrows and the rounded, ribbed back of the bowl
shows. Same style rules as logo-ideas/codex-brief-3.md: no faces, no text,
monoline outline in `currentColor`, round joins and caps, the lively tilt of
Monika's bow (the reference Lunara has), 512 viewBox, reads at 16 px.

Starting points drawn by Claude (look at them, then do better):
logo-ideas/lute-f.svg (turned lute: narrowed face plus a second curve for
the back), lute-g.svg (one rib on the back), lute-h.svg (bold line, the back
reads as a crescent). The flat views are lute-a..e.svg.

## What makes it a lute
A pear-shaped bowl body, a carved rose (sound hole) on the face, a bridge
low on the face, a fairly short wide neck, and the **pegbox bent back at a
sharp angle** (nearly 90 degrees) at the top of the neck. In three-quarter
view the bowl's back bulges beyond the face on one side, and the pegbox's
bend is the most distinctive silhouette cue.

## Deliverables
Six concepts, logo-ideas/lute3q-1.svg … lute3q-6.svg, exploring different
answers, for example: the back as one sweeping curve; the back as two or
three ribs fanning from the neck joint; the back as a solid filled crescent
with the face as an outline; a steeper or shallower turn; the neck running
toward the viewer with a foreshortened pegbox; the rose as a small star
(her seed's hat ornament) or a simple ring. Use teal #1f6f78 or warm red
#d6455a via the root svg's `color`. Each under ~2 KB, no external refs.
Add logo-ideas/lute3q.md with a name and one line each. Render at 512, 32
and 16 px and simplify any that clog when small.
