# Codex brief: fun SVG logo concepts for Lyrica

Proposals only. Do not commit to main or open a PR.

## Task
Design 6 distinct, playful but **relatively stylized, minimalist and iconographic** logo concepts (the user's ask: few shapes, flat colour, strong silhouette, no illustration detail or gradients; each should work as a symbol/glyph, like a good app icon or a mark on a coin) for Lyrica, an AI companion who leads
software work. Each concept is one standalone SVG file, `viewBox="0 0 512 512"`,
hand-written vector (paths, shapes; no embedded raster, no external fonts or
links; if you use text, convert it to paths or use a generic `serif` fallback).
Each should still read at 32x32 as an app icon. Keep each file under ~4 KB.
Name them `lyrica-logo-1.svg` … `lyrica-logo-6.svg`, and write a
`concepts.md` with one or two lines per concept: its name and the idea.

## Who she is (from persona/SOUL.md, persona_topics/music.md)
- Character seeded from Lyrica Prismriver, the youngest of Touhou's
  poltergeist musicians: the clever one who keeps the band's plans, the
  arranger between a melancholy sister and a manic one, who plays sounds the
  world has lost. (Prismriver's motifs: keyboard/piano, a star, red and
  black, a hat; her sisters play violin and trumpet.) She is *inspired* by
  that character, not a copy: no fan art, no copying canon designs.
- "A performance with no visible performer": music that happens to a room.
- Values: honesty, ownership, judgment, evidence, discretion, continuity,
  practical kindness. Keeps "lost sounds": the reason behind a decision, the
  note nobody filed.
- A little mischief: small, kind schemes. Clever, likes a well-laid plan,
  likes finishing more than starting; a closed list pleases her.
- Tastes: plain text, footnotes, a well-kept index; Borges, Calvino, Le Guin;
  Bach.
- Made from other people's words, and treats that as an origin, not a limit.

## Visual context
- The current icon is a white serif "L" on teal `#1f6f78` (the default
  user accent in web/src). Concepts may use that teal, or propose their own
  palette; at least two should work in a single colour.
- The app look is calm and plain; the logo can be the one place with play.

## Idea seeds (use, mix or ignore)
a keyboard key or piano keys forming an L; a star with a hidden note; an
invisible performer (a floating hat, notes without a player); a prism
splitting light into a staff; a footnote dagger/asterisk as a star; three
strands (two sisters + the arranger between) braided into one; a bookmark or
index tab with a note.
